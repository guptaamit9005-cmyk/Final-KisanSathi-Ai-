Good Seed Indicators

Plump, uniform size, lustrous seed coat, heavy weight (sinks in water), and no black embryo points.

❌

Bad Seed Warning Signs

Insect bore holes (घुन), musty damp smell, discolored purple/black fungal spots, hollow floating seeds.

🛡️

Why Seed Treatment?

Treating seeds with Beejamrit, Trichoderma, or Thiram protects seedlings against root rot, wilt, and damping off.
