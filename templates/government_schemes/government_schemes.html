🛡️ Farmer Assistance & Guidance for Government Applications

1. Verify Official Portals

Always apply through official portals ending with .gov.in or .nic.in. KisanSathi AI links exclusively to authentic government portals.

2. Common Service Centres (CSC)

If you lack high-speed internet or a printer, visit your nearest Village Common Service Centre (Jan Seva Kendra) with your Aadhaar and Land Khasra records.

3. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar card and enabled for NPCI mapping so that financial assistance is credited directly without delays.

4. Kisan Call Centre Helpline

For any nationwide agricultural queries or grievance filing, dial the government toll-free Kisan Call Centre at 1800-180-1551 (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM).
